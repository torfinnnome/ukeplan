Apple Integrations for Week Planner

Since your entire family uses Apple devices, you can forward school plans directly from the native Share Sheet in Mail, Safari, or Files, as well as subscribe directly in Apple Calendar across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

1. "Share to Week Planner" (Apple Shortcut)

Allows sharing any PDF or camera photo directly into Week Planner

How to create the shortcut in Apple Shortcuts (iPhone / iPad):

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → tap + in the top-right corner → New Shortcut.
  2. Tap the shortcut name at the top of the screen to open the shortcut details, then:
    • Set the name to 'Share to Week Planner' (and pick an icon if you like).
    • Turn on Show in Share Sheet.
    • Under Input → Receive: tick Files and Images.
  3. Back in the editor, tap + (bottom-right) → search for 'HTTP' → add the action Send HTTP Request:
    • http://...
    • POST
    • file  → 
    • channel  →  "shortcut"
  4. Optional: add the action Show Notification with the text 'Week Planner updated!'.
  5. Tap Done. You can now share: open any PDF or photo in Mail, Files, or Photos → Share → 'Share to Week Planner'.

2. Subscription in Apple Calendar

View all activities and gear reminders on lock screens, Apple Watch, and widgets

Calendar subscription link:

On iPhone: Go to Settings → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar and paste the URL above.

3. Email Forwarding (IMAP Bot)

Forward emails from schools, teachers, or clubs

The background bot monitors the mailbox configured in your environment variables:

IMAP_ENABLED=true
IMAP_HOST=imap.gmail.com
IMAP_USER=family-weekplan@gmail.com
IMAP_PASSWORD=your-app-password
IMAP_POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS=120

When you receive an email containing a week schedule (as a PDF attachment or directly in the email body), simply forward it to this address. The bot parses it and updates the dashboard automatically.

4. Share with the rest of the family

Create the shortcut once — everyone else imports it with one tap

Once the shortcut exists on one device, share it with the family:

  1. Open the shortcut in the Shortcuts app → tap the share icon (top-right) → choose Share via iCloud → Copy Link.
  2. Send the link to the family (Messages, Mail, …). When a family member taps the link, Shortcuts opens with an Add Shortcut button — one tap and it is in their share sheet.
  3. Alternatively: choose Share via AirDrop and pick the other iPhone/iPad directly.
